About Us

Built on Engineering Discipline

Qilestra Tech is a managed service provider that treats infrastructure as a craft. We design, deploy, and document enterprise IT systems with the rigor they demand.

Who We Are

Qilestra Tech was founded on a straightforward premise: organizations deserve IT partners who can execute at an enterprise level without the overhead of a large consultancy.

We are a small, focused team of infrastructure engineers and security practitioners. Our engagements span Azure cloud architecture, Active Directory environments, SIEM deployments, and Linux server fleets. Every project we deliver includes full technical documentation, architecture diagrams, and runbooks — because infrastructure without documentation is technical debt.

We work with mid-market companies and growing enterprises that need hands-on engineering, not slide decks. Our clients typically come to us when they need to modernize identity systems, stand up security monitoring, harden legacy environments, or migrate workloads to Azure.

How We Work

Every engagement follows a structured methodology:

  • Discovery & Assessment — We audit the current environment, identify gaps, and define scope.
  • Architecture & Design — We produce architecture documents and implementation plans before writing a single line of configuration.
  • Implementation — We build in phases with validation checkpoints at each stage.
  • Documentation & Handoff — We deliver runbooks, configuration references, and knowledge transfer sessions.
